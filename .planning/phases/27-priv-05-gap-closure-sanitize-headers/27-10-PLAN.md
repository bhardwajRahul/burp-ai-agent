<objective>
Close the underscore name class at the REGEX, in the safe direction, and correct the claim that hid it.

`Redaction.isCookieHeaderName` is a bare `contains("cookie")`. Its two REDACTING consumers are safe
when it is wider than the regexes. Its third consumer,
`PassiveAiScannerFilters.sanitizeHeadersForPrompt:186`, is an **ADMITTER** — a `true` result puts the
header into the outbound prompt — and there wider is fail-**OPEN**. `COOKIE_NAME_PART` excludes `_`,
which is a legal RFC 9110 tchar, so the difference set is non-empty, reachable, and was measured
leaking a cookie value to a third-party AI backend under STRICT and under BALANCED.

Purpose: the maintainer decided FIX rather than ACCEPT, and decided the direction — widen the regex
side, never narrow the predicate. Narrowing the predicate would shrink what `sanitizeHeaders` strips
on the MCP path, which is the direction that reopened this phase in the first place.

Output: a widened name class, an inverted pin, a corrected claim at both sites a reader meets it, and
a machine check over the character axis with the uncovered remainder enumerated in source.

**WHAT THIS PLAN MUST NOT DO.** It must not narrow `isCookieHeaderName`. It must not drop `my_cookie`
from the parity corpus — the entry is the measurement and it stays, with its assertion inverted. It
must not touch `hostHeaderRegex`, `authHeaderRegex`'s name alternation, `InjectionPointExtractor.kt`
or anything on the `AuditIssue.detail()` route: those are `AR-27-04`, `CONCERNS.md` W-A and Phase 28
respectively, each already owned. And it must not tick `REQUIREMENTS.md`.
