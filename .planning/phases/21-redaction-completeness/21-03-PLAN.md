<objective>
Close the second caller-side OFF bypass (D-06, MCP half) and correct the four user-facing strings that
claim `PrivacyMode.OFF` means no redaction (D-07 as amended).

D-05 makes OFF mean *"no built-in redaction"* rather than *"no redaction at all"* — a user-visible
semantic change. D-07 travels with D-05 rather than deferring to DOC-03 for the reason Phase 20's D-12
established: leaving a user-facing claim false for five phases is worse than a small overlap with the
docs phase. The strings and the behaviour are one decision; shipping one without the other misdescribes
the product.

The `McpToolContext` deletion is behaviour-preserving today — `RedactionPolicy.fromMode(OFF)` sets all
three flags false, so `Redaction.apply` returns its input byte-identically — and becomes load-bearing
when plan 21-06 moves the custom-pattern loop outside the `redactTokens` branch in wave 3.

Purpose: PRIV-05 (remove a bypass on a live leak path), PRIV-06 / D-05 / D-07 (make the product's own
description of OFF true).
Output: one deleted branch and four corrected strings, three of them conditioned on whether custom
patterns are configured.

**Out of scope, deliberately:** `ui/panels/HelpConfigPanel.kt:26` (`"OFF (raw data)"`) stays with
DOC-03 / Phase 26 — it is a mode summary rather than a redaction claim and sits in a panel Phase 26
rewrites. The GitBook privacy page and the SEC-04 / PRIV-05 security advisory also stay with DOC-03.
</objective>
