<objective>
Close **WR-02** of `21-REVIEW.md`, deliberately deferred by plan 21-12 and still open.
`SettingsPanelActions.kt:243` infers "custom patterns are applied" from `customPatternsArea.text` —
the live `JTextArea`, which is not the engine's state. `Redaction.compiledCustomPatterns` is only
updated by `applyAndSaveSettings`, and `validateAndCollectCustomPatterns` drops any line failing
`isPatternSafe`. A user mid-typing in OFF mode, or one who has typed only patterns that will be
rejected, sees the reassuring clause while raw traffic is in fact flowing to MCP and prompts.

The banner downgrades a real risk warning on the strength of text that has no effect on anything. Its
two sibling call sites — `ChatPanel.kt:315` and `ChatPanel.kt:1153` — both already read
`getSettings().customRedactionPatterns`, the persisted and validated list. Only this one diverges,
which makes it a defect rather than a design choice.

Purpose: **D-07** put these strings in scope precisely because leaving a user-facing privacy claim
false is worse than a small overlap with the docs phase, and **D-05** is what makes the claim
load-bearing — under OFF, the user's custom patterns are the *only* redaction there is. A banner that
says they are active when they are not is the highest-consequence version of that error.

Output: the composition moved out of the Swing extension into a top-level pure function whose
parameter is the persisted list, so the defect becomes unrepresentable rather than merely fixed; plus
a test file pinning every arm.

This plan touches no file owned by any other plan in this round.
</objective>
