<objective>
Close **CR-01 of `21-REVIEW-2.md`**: CR-02 is not closed. `isJsonPairBoundaryRisk` only detects a
window cut sitting in the `\s*` *around the colon*. It cannot detect a cut sitting *inside an open
quoted value*, because `jsonSecretKeyRegex`'s value alternative is `"[^"]*"` and `[^"]*` matches
newlines. When the value's opening quote is on one line and its closing quote is on the next, the
window's last line ends with an ordinary value character, the lookahead never starts, the cap is
irrelevant, and the pair is cut in half. Neither half matches, so the value is emitted verbatim.

The reviewer drove the **compiled shipped `Redaction` object** over 40 boundary alignments of a 1 MB
body under `PrivacyMode.STRICT`: **6 alignments leak with `dropMarker=false`, while the single-pass
control does not leak.** `dropMarker=false` is the load-bearing part — this is a leak, not a
fail-closed drop. It is reachable in the default configuration through
`McpToolContext.redactIfNeeded`, whose `maxBodyBytes` default is 2 MiB, twice
`MAX_REDACTION_BODY_CHARS`.

Purpose: this is the same statement that made CR-02 a blocker in `21-REVIEW.md` — a redaction bypass
keyed only on payload size and alignment, on attacker-controlled content, at a deterministic cut.
PRIV-06 is not met while it stands.

Output: a widened risk predicate that models the state `[^"]*` is actually in; a third boundary sweep
guarding the shape the existing two structurally cannot reach; and the three records that currently
overclaim coverage corrected in the same pass.

Locked decisions in force: **D-01 AMENDED** (cut at line boundaries, no overlap — the overlap clause
stays dropped on its measured ground), **D-02** (fail closed), **D-08 REFINED** (a record claims only
what ships).
</objective>
