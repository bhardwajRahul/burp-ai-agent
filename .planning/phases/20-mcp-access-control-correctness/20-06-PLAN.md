This plan runs alone in wave 4 and produces no source change. It exists as a separate serialised plan
for one reason: the experiment **transiently overwrites**
`src/main/kotlin/com/six2dez/burp/aiagent/mcp/KtorMcpServerManager.kt` with its pre-phase content. If it
ran inside wave 3 alongside `20-05`, that plan's `McpSupervisorProbeTest` would execute against the
rolled-back manager and fail spuriously, and its `git status` verification would be polluted. Serialising
removes the hazard rather than documenting it.

covered by the same run).

</objective>
